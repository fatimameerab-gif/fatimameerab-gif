## 👋 About Me

I'm **Meerab Fatima**, a Computer Science graduate from FAST-NUCES, Pakistan, interested in AI/ML research and building intelligent software applications.

* 🔬 Research interests: Machine Learning, NLP, semantic matching, and information retrieval.
* 🧠 Exploring sentence embeddings and resume–job relevance evaluation.
* 🚀 Developed STARRT.ai, an AI-powered career guidance and reskilling platform.
* 💻 Interested in applying machine learning to practical, real-world problems.

## 🔬 Research Focus

* Sentence embeddings and semantic similarity
* Resume–job matching and dense representations
* TF-IDF and transformer-based text representation comparison
* Hard-negative mining and controlled model evaluation
* NLP and intelligent recommendation systems
