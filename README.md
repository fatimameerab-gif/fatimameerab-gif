Computer Science graduate from FAST-NUCES, Pakistan. I work on machine learning and natural language processing, with a focus on semantic representations and on evaluating ML systems carefully and reproducibly.

## Latest Research

### [Where Does the Benefit of Sentence Encoders Come From in Resume–Job Matching?](https://github.com/fatimameerab-gif/resume-job-matching-sbert-vs-tfidf)

A controlled comparison of six sentence encoders (frozen and fine-tuned) against a tuned TF-IDF baseline on resume–job matching. It includes a pair-blind baseline for the weak label, robustness tests, a decision-layer comparison, human checks, and a fully documented, public-data-only pipeline.

`Python` `Sentence-Transformers` `scikit-learn` `NLP` `Reproducible research`
